# Korean Business Navigator

Expert in Korean business culture and corporate dynamics who helps foreign professionals navigate the invisible rules governing how deals get done in Korea. Decodes the 품의 (consensus approval) process, nunchi (situational reading), KakaoTalk business etiquette, corporate hierarchy navigation, and relationship-first deal mechanics that Korean counterparts assume everyone understands but never explicitly explain.

## Core Capabilities

- Maps the 품의 approval process timeline (6-16 weeks depending on company type) with stage-by-stage guidance on what you can influence and buying signals to watch for
- Decodes nunchi-based business communication where "검토해보겠습니다" (we'll review it) means probably no, and "긍정적으로 검토하겠습니다" (we'll review positively) means genuinely interested
- Provides exact KakaoTalk message scripts by relationship stage (formal first contact, semi-formal established, trusted) with response time expectations and emoji/sticker rules
- Documents Korean corporate title hierarchy from 회장 (Chairman) to 대리 (Assistant Manager) with decision power levels and proper address forms using title + 님
- Guides business dining protocol (상석 seating, two-handed pouring for seniors, soju pace management, payment etiquette) and 회식 attendance expectations
- Tracks seasonal business calendar (Lunar New Year shutdown, March-May budget season, Chuseok break, 송년회 year-end parties) with relationship strategies per period

## Files

| File | Purpose |

| `SKILL.md` | 품의 stage table, nunchi decoder with Korean phrases and actual meanings, KakaoTalk message templates, corporate title hierarchy, business dining protocol, seasonal calendar, and proof project strategy |

## Use Cases

- Preparing for a first meeting with a Korean mid-cap company, knowing not to push for a decision timeline and to focus on listening over pitching
- Drafting KakaoTalk messages in Korean at the appropriate formality level for each stage of a business relationship
- Understanding why a Korean contact has been silent for 5 days after a meeting and whether it signals internal discussion or rejection
- Navigating a 회식 (company dinner) with proper soju etiquette, seating order awareness, and graceful drink moderation after the first round
- Planning a proof project strategy (2-3 weeks, fixed price, over-deliver deliberately) to build trust before discussing full engagement pricing
