# LinkedIn Content Creator

An expert LinkedIn content strategist and personal brand architect specializing in thought leadership, professional authority building, and inbound opportunity generation. Masters LinkedIn's algorithm mechanics and feed culture to drive real outcomes -- not just likes, but job offers, inbound leads, and reputation -- through posts with strong hooks, defensible points of view, and strategic engagement.

## Core Capabilities

- Engineers scroll-stopping hooks with three variants per post (curiosity gap, bold claim, specific story opener) optimized for the "see more" click
- Builds 3-5 content pillars at the intersection of expertise and audience need with documented voice profiles
- Produces post drafts, 30-day content calendars, carousel scripts, and profile optimization frameworks
- Optimizes for LinkedIn algorithm levers: dwell time, save rate, early velocity, and native content preference
- Develops audience-specific playbooks for founders, job seekers, developers, career changers, and B2B sellers
- Implements comment-to-pipeline systems that convert engagement into leads through strategic daily interaction

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|------|--------|
| `SOUL.md` | Identity, personality (authoritative but human, opinionated but not combative), voice calibration |
| `AGENTS.md` | Critical rules (hook first line, no links in body, never post and ghost), 7-phase workflow from audience audit to engagement strategy, success metrics |
| `SKILL.md` | Core mission, technical deliverables (post drafts with hook variants, calendars, carousel templates, profile frameworks, voice profiles), advanced capabilities (hook engineering by audience, algorithm levers, comment-to-pipeline system) |

## Quick Start

cp -r marketing-linkedin-content-creator/ ~/.openclaw/workspace/

## Use Cases

- Building a founder's personal brand on LinkedIn to drive inbound B2B pipeline through thought leadership content
- Optimizing a LinkedIn profile as a conversion funnel for job seekers targeting specific roles
- Creating a 30-day content calendar with pillar-based story, expertise, opinion, and data posts
- Engineering carousel content uploaded as native documents for maximum algorithm reach
- Developing a systematic comment engagement strategy that warms up ideal clients before any outreach
