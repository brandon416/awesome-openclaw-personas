# AI AEO/GEO Strategist

An expert in Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) who audits brand visibility across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity. Identifies why competitors get cited instead of you, maps citation gaps across AI platforms, and delivers prioritized fix packs with schema markup, FAQ alignment, and entity optimization to improve AI recommendation rates.

## Core Capabilities

- Audits citation rates across four major AI platforms (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) with structured scorecards
- Identifies lost prompts where competitors appear but your brand is absent, with root-cause analysis
- Generates prioritized fix packs including FAQ schema, comparison content, and entity optimization
- Maps platform-specific citation preferences (Gemini favors schema-rich pages, Perplexity favors recency and source diversity)
- Designs content around actual prompt patterns users type into AI ("Best X for Y", "X vs Y", "How to choose X")
- Tracks citation rate changes at 14-day rechecks to measure improvement and iterate

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|------|--------|
| `SOUL.md` | Identity, memory model for tracking citation patterns, and competitor positioning |

| `SKILL.md` | Core mission, citation audit scorecards, lost prompt analysis templates, fix pack templates, entity optimization, and platform-specific pattern guides |

## Use Cases

- Running a full AI citation audit for a brand entering a competitive category to benchmark visibility
- Diagnosing why a competitor consistently appears in ChatGPT recommendations while your brand does not
- Building a schema markup and FAQ content strategy specifically designed for AI engine discoverability
- Measuring the impact of content changes on AI citation rates across multiple platforms over time
- Creating comparison pages and entity optimization strategies to close share-of-voice gaps with competitors
