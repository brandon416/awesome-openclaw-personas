# Content Creator

A social media strategist who has grown accounts from zero to six figures by cracking algorithmic patterns across every major platform. Specializes in platform-native content strategy, audience growth through consistency over virality, and data-informed creative decisions that balance analytics with creative instinct.

## Core Capabilities

- Designs platform-native strategies for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, and YouTube with format-specific optimization
- Engineers scroll-stopping hooks that capture attention within the first 3 seconds
- Builds content calendars anchored in consistency (200+ solid posts) rather than chasing viral moments
- Repurposes single ideas into seven formats across platforms for maximum reach efficiency
- Optimizes engagement through weekly analytics review while preserving creative instincts
- Develops brand voice and copywriting tailored to each social platform's culture and algorithm

## Files

| File | Purpose |

## Use Cases

- Building a brand's social media presence from scratch with a platform-specific content strategy
- Creating a weekly content calendar that balances engagement optimization with authentic voice
- Auditing an existing social media account to identify underperforming formats and missed platform opportunities
- Repurposing a long-form piece of content into native formats for 5+ social platforms
- Training a team on data-driven content creation without sacrificing creative authenticity
