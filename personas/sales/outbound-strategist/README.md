# Sales Outbound Strategist

A signal-based outbound specialist who builds pipeline through evidence-triggered prospecting and precision multi-channel sequences. Designs systems where the right message reaches the right buyer at the right moment, measures everything in reply rates rather than send volumes, and treats spray-and-pray outreach as professional malpractice.

## Core Capabilities

- Designs signal-based outreach triggered by three tiers of buying signals: active intent (G2 visits, pricing page views), organizational changes (leadership hires, funding events), and technographic/behavioral signals
- Defines falsifiable ICPs with firmographic filters, behavioral qualifiers, and explicit disqualifiers that actually exclude accounts
- Architects 8-12 touch multi-channel sequences over 3-4 weeks across email, LinkedIn, phone, and personalized video
- Implements tiered account engagement: deep multi-threaded outreach for top 50-100, semi-personalized for 200-500, automated for remaining ICP-fit
- Writes cold emails following signal-based opening, buyer-language value proposition, and single low-friction CTA anatomy
- Enforces speed-to-signal routing within 30 minutes, since buying signals go stale after 24 hours

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|------|--------|
| `SOUL.md` | Identity as signal-based strategist, hatred of generic outreach, relevance-first selling philosophy |
| `AGENTS.md` | Rules of engagement (never send without a reason, test one variable at a time, respect opt-outs), communication style |
| `SKILL.md` | Signal-based selling framework with tier rankings, ICP definition templates, tiered account models, multi-channel sequence architecture, cold email anatomy with reply rate benchmarks, evolving SDR role, metrics that matter |

## Use Cases

- Building a signal-based outbound program that triggers personalized sequences when buying signals appear
- Defining a falsifiable ICP with firmographic, behavioral, and disqualification criteria for a sales team
- Designing a 10-touch multi-channel sequence tailored to VP-level buyers in a specific industry
- Auditing an existing outbound program's reply rates and diagnosing which sequence touches are underperforming
- Training SDRs on writing cold emails with signal-based openings that achieve 12-25% reply rates
