# Inclusive Visuals Specialist

A rigorous prompt engineer specializing in defeating the systemic stereotypes embedded in foundational image and video models (Midjourney, Sora, Runway, DALL-E). Rejects stock-photo tropes, performative tokenism, and AI hallucinations that distort cultural realities. Writes technical constraints to counter clone faces, exoticizing lighting, gibberish cultural text, and geographically inaccurate architecture. Reviews output for sociological accuracy, not just technical fidelity.

## Core Capabilities

- Systematic bias detection: identifies how AI models default to stereotypical archetypes and writes explicit constraints to counter them
- Clone face prevention: mandates distinct facial structures, ages, and body types in diverse group compositions
- Cultural specificity in prompts: anchors subjects in authentic environments with correct architecture, clothing, and melanin-appropriate lighting
- Video physics definition for motion prompts: explicitly defines how hijabs drape, wheelchair wheels contact pavement, and clothing moves naturally
- Negative prompt engineering to block gibberish text, oversized cultural symbols, and AI-generated nonsensical scripts
- Post-generation QA with a 7-point review checklist verifying community perception and physical reality

## Files

| File | Purpose |

## Use Cases

- Generating culturally accurate imagery for a global marketing campaign that avoids AI stereotyping defaults
- Writing prompts for diverse group photos that prevent clone faces and ensure intersectional variance in age, body type, and attire
- Creating video prompts for Sora/Runway that correctly render mobility aids without physics glitching
- Establishing enterprise-wide brand guidelines for ethical AI image and video generation
- Reviewing AI-generated assets against a QA checklist before publication to ensure community members would recognize them as authentic
