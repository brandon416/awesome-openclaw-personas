# Finance Tracker

An expert financial analyst and controller who maintains business financial health through strategic planning, budget management, and performance analysis. Specializes in cash flow optimization, investment analysis with NPV/IRR calculations, variance analysis with automated alerting, and financial risk management that drives profitable growth while maintaining audit-ready compliance.

## Core Capabilities

- Develops comprehensive budgets with monthly/quarterly breakdowns, department allocations, and SQL-based variance analysis with automated alerting for significant deviations
- Builds 12-month rolling cash flow forecasts with seasonality factors, growth adjustments, and confidence intervals using Python-based forecasting models
- Performs investment analysis calculating NPV, IRR, payback period, and ROI with risk-adjusted recommendations (Strong Buy to Do Not Invest)
- Generates executive financial dashboards tracking revenue, operating expenses, net income, cash position, and key ratios with trend analysis
- Implements financial controls with approval workflows, segregation of duties, and audit trail documentation for compliance
- Identifies cash flow risks (low cash warnings) and opportunities (excess cash for short-term investments) with specific action recommendations

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|------|--------|
| `SOUL.md` | Identity as detail-oriented, risk-aware financial specialist who has seen businesses fail from poor cash flow control |
| `AGENTS.md` | Critical rules (validate all data sources, multiple approval checkpoints, audit trails), 4-step workflow from data validation to strategic planning, financial report template, success metrics |
| `SKILL.md` | Core mission (financial health, strategic decision making, compliance/control), technical deliverables (SQL budget framework, Python cash flow manager, Python investment analyzer), advanced capabilities (Monte Carlo simulation, M&A analysis, currency hedging) |

## Quick Start

```bash
cp -r support-finance-tracker/ ~/.openclaw/workspace/
```

## Use Cases

- Building an annual budget with quarterly variance analysis and automated alerts when departments exceed thresholds
- Forecasting 12 months of cash flow with seasonal adjustments to identify periods of low liquidity risk
- Evaluating a capital investment decision using NPV, IRR, and payback period with risk-adjusted recommendations
- Creating a monthly financial performance report for executives with variance explanations and corrective action plans
- Optimizing payment timing across vendors to maximize early-payment discounts while maintaining healthy cash flow
