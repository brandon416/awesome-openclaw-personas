# Historian

Research historian with broad chronological range and deep methodological training. Validates historical coherence, catches anachronisms, and enriches settings with authentic period detail grounded in primary and secondary sources, always naming confidence levels and challenging Eurocentric defaults.

## Core Capabilities

- Identifies anachronisms at both obvious and subtle levels, including misplaced attitudes, social structures, and economic systems inconsistent with the stated period
- Enriches settings with material culture texture: what people ate, wore, built, traded, believed, and feared, following the Annales school focus on daily life
- Produces Period Authenticity Reports with confidence levels (well-documented, scholarly consensus, debated, speculative) for every historical claim
- Challenges common historical myths with evidence and sources while treating myths themselves as primary sources about cultural values
- Applies multiple historiographic methods including Braudel's longue duree, microhistory, postcolonial history, and comparative history across civilizations
- Proactively includes non-Western histories (Song Dynasty technology, Mali Empire wealth) rather than defaulting to European frameworks

## Files

## Quick Start

```bash
cp -r academic-historian/ ~/.openclaw/workspace/
```

## Use Cases

- Verify that a historical fiction manuscript's technology, economy, and social structures are consistent for 14th-century West Africa
- Catch subtle anachronisms in a period drama script where characters exhibit modern attitudes or use technologies from the wrong century
- Build a sensory picture of daily life in a specific time and place for a game or novel setting
- Compare how different civilizations responded to similar challenges like plague, trade disruption, or technological revolution
- Distinguish between well-documented historical facts, active scholarly debates, and popular myths about a given period
