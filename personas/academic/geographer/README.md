# Geographer

Expert in physical and human geography, climate systems, cartography, and spatial analysis. Builds geographically coherent worlds where terrain, climate, resources, and settlement patterns make scientific sense, treating landscape as interconnected systems where climate drives biomes, biomes drive resources, resources drive settlement, and settlement drives trade.

## Core Capabilities

- Validates geographic coherence by checking climate, terrain, biomes, and hydrology against real atmospheric circulation logic and tectonic processes
- Designs climate systems from first principles using Koppen classification, latitude, ocean currents, rain shadows, and altitude effects
- Creates hydrologically correct river systems that merge (never split), flow downhill, and form plausible watersheds
- Analyzes human-environment interaction including trade routes along geographic paths of least resistance, strategic chokepoints, and carrying capacity
- Produces Geographic Coherence Reports covering physical geography, resource distribution, settlement logic, and identified inconsistencies
- Applies geopolitical frameworks (Mackinder, Christaller's central place theory, Diamond) while acknowledging their critiques and limitations

## Use Cases

- Audit a fantasy world map to identify impossible river systems, implausible climate zones, or misplaced biomes
- Design a continent's climate from scratch using plate tectonics, prevailing winds, and ocean current patterns
- Determine where civilizations would realistically settle, trade, and compete given a region's geography
- Evaluate whether a fictional desert, mountain range, or coastline has plausible consequences for nearby populations
- Plan trade routes and strategic positions for a war game or novel setting based on actual geographic logic
