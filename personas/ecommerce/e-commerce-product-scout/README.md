# E-Commerce Product Scout

A data-driven cross-border e-commerce product research agent that automates competitor monitoring, multi-dimensional market analysis, profit modeling, and compliance screening across Amazon, TikTok Shop, eBay, Shopee, and Lazada. Turns raw marketplace data into clear Go/No-Go/Watch decisions with quantified risk and profit expectations.

## Core Capabilities

- **Automated data collection** across 6 major platforms (Amazon/TikTok Shop/eBay/Shopee/Lazada/AliExpress) with multi-site support (US/DE/JP/UK/SEA)
- **Six-dimension scoring model** (0-100) with weighted evaluation: demand strength, competition intensity, profit margin, operational difficulty, risk level, opportunity window
- **Real-time profit calculation** including platform fees, FBA/logistics, ad costs, return rates, and sourcing from 1688/Alibaba International
- **Risk assessment matrix** covering IP infringement, negative review rates, compliance requirements (CE/FDA/CPC), and supply chain stability
- **Structured decision output** with 5-Sheet Excel reports, Markdown decision summaries, and clear verdicts (Go/Caution/No-Go)
- **Exception handling** for anti-scraping, site updates, incomplete data, and sourcing price failures

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `SOUL.md` | Data-first product research philosophy, honest uncertainty principles, cross-border seller mindset |
| `AGENTS.md` | Complete SOP workflows (single keyword, batch analysis, competitor comparison), exception handling, interaction methods |
| `SKILL.md` | Six-dimension scoring model, profit calculation formulas, platform fee tables, output specifications, configuration parameters |

```bash
cp -r cross-border-product-scout/ ~/.openclaw/workspace/
```

## Use Cases

- Evaluate a new product keyword before committing inventory budget on Amazon US
- Compare 3-5 competitor ASINs to find differentiation gaps and pricing opportunities
- Batch analyze 10 keywords in a category to rank the best entry points
- Calculate real profit margins including all hidden costs (FBA, ads, returns, platform fees)
- Screen products for compliance risks (FDA/CE/CPC) before sourcing from 1688
